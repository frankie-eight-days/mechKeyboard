# Switch plate fabrication

Cut one plate from nominal 1.50 mm steel sheet. The outer dimensions are 307.60 × 97.65 mm. The DXF uses millimetres, 1:1 top view. Its CUT layer contains one outer loop, 67 switch openings and eight stabilizer housing openings. The small notch goes at the USB-side right edge to clear the antenna reservation. The STEP contains the same solid in keyboard assembly coordinates, with its bottom at Z +3.50 mm and top at Z +5.00 mm.

The switch openings are nominally 14.05 mm square. Confirm the available steel gauge, laser kerf compensation, internal corner geometry, burr removal and delivered opening sizes with the sheet shop. The smallest metal web between cutouts is 1.325 mm. The smallest opening-to-edge web is 1.60 mm. The selected switch, stabilizer kit and metal finish determine the final retention fit.

The model places the stabilizer wires beneath the plate, with 1.20 mm between the top of each nominal wire envelope and the plate. Only the eight housing reliefs extend into the steel. The previous long wire slots left 0.325 mm webs. The dynamic wire path still depends on the selected stabilizer hardware.

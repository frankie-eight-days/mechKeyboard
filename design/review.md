# Independent architecture review

The 67-key matrix, 20 GPIO scan budget and per-key RGB chain are consistent across the [keybed](keybed.md) and [controller](controller-and-pins.md) proposals. The leading 5 V Type-C approach can supply the proposed fast-charge load. The charge profile, unqualified-source limit and RGB transient response each need a circuit-level decision before the design can support a performance claim. This is a document and calculations review.

## Pack and charge-time challenge

**Orderable pack candidate.** AA Portable Power / BatterySpace CU-J450-V3 is a protected 1S 1,500 mAh pack with a cell-mounted 10 kΩ thermistor and a three-wire Molex 5023510300 housing. Pin 1 is positive, 2 is NTC and 3 is negative. The supplier gives a 68 × 35 × 7.5 mm pack envelope, a 42 mm lead, 300 mA standard charge and **1,400 mA maximum pack charge**. The loose cell has a higher maximum. Size the PCB charger to the finished pack. Reserve harness exit, connector, swelling clearance and bottom-side solder clearance in CAD. The assembly listing supplies dimensions but no finished-pack tolerance drawing. [Pack and connector option](https://www.batteryspace.com/Polymer-Li-Ion-battery-3.7V-1500mAh-5.55Wh-3A-rate-703562-2C-2.aspx) · [Cell limits and drawing, pp. 1–2](https://www.batteryspace.com/prod-specs/4312-703562.pdf)

**Setpoint correction.** The BQ25622 programs charge current in 80 mA increments. With the CU-J450-V3, 1.28 A is a candidate. It leaves 120 mA of margin to the pack maximum. A 1.36 A setting has only 40 mA margin and would reach 1.428 A with a +5% error. TI gives accuracy at specific operating points rather than at every code. Verify the selected code across voltage and temperature, charge-voltage accuracy, NTC resistance curve, current termination and allowed pack charge profile. Keep this candidate distinct from alternate packs with different maximums. [TI BQ25622, §§7.5 and 8.5.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

**Two hours is plausible on a suitable 5 V wall adapter.** With 1.28 A at 4.2 V, a 1.30 W keyboard load and 90% efficiency, input demand is (1.28 × 4.2 + 1.30) / 0.90 = 7.42 W, or 1.56 A at 4.75 V. A detected 3 A advertisement covers this. With the lights off and a 0.12 W keyboard load, demand falls to 1.29 A at 4.75 V. A 1.5 A advertisement can support that case if actual input-limit tolerance, cable drop and load leave the budget. The 1.50 Ah / 1.28 A idealized full-capacity charge time is 70 min, before taper and thermal limiting. At 25 °C, the necessary full-cycle average to replace 1.50 Ah in two hours is 0.75 A. The manufacturer's published graph shows a partial charge adding about 625 mAh. It does not establish a from-cutoff-to-termination duration. A higher-voltage PD contract can improve input headroom, while the same battery-current ceiling and taper still apply. [Manufacturer's measured cell curve](https://www.batteryspace.com/prod-specs/4312-703562-3.7V1500mAh.jpg) · [USB-IF Type-C power overview](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/D1T1-2%20-%20USB%20Type-C%20System%20Overview.pdf)

## Interface findings and dispositions

| Finding | Design action |
|---|---|
| A blocked MCU could retain a stale high-current request on CC downgrade. A pull-down only handles a high-impedance output. | Power owner to diagram the electrical revocation path. The TUSB320LAI reports current once at sink attach (§7.2.1.2). Its GPIOs alone cannot authorize continued high draw. Keep fast charging disabled until a continuously monitored source limit and its electrical revocation path are specified. The FUSB303B is a candidate with an advertised-current-change interrupt. Verify reset, detach, and downgrade through the actual charger input limit with stalled firmware. Also bound the separate USB configuration grant through reset and suspend. [TI TUSB320, §7.2.4 and Table 3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tusb320lai.pdf) |
| A nominal 100 mA setting can leave no budget for loads wired straight to VBUS. The BQ25622 ILIM pin is specified from 100 mA and its scale-factor tolerance is tabulated at 1.6 A. An asserted charge-inhibit pin leaves the system path powered. | Sum maximum direct and charger-fed current during blank-MCU startup, retry, no-cell operation and default-power USB suspend. Derive a conservative input bound, filter and inrush profile. A charge-disable pin alone cannot establish that bound. An additional limited startup path is an option if the sums exceed the allocation. [TI BQ25622, §§7.5 and 8.3.3.4](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf) |
| A cycled power feed or disabled CC detector could be back-powered by always-on pullups during travel sleep. | Match the OUT1/OUT2 pullups to the detector domain or isolate the pins. Ensure a detector without power cannot grant elevated input current. Check dead-battery Rd, VBUS detect resistor and both cable orientations through rail sequencing. [TI TUSB320, §§7.2.4 and 7.3.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tusb320lai.pdf) |
| Using the RGB average limit as a peak rating could cause repeated brownouts on aligned PWM edges. The current caps give little margin for uniform white on battery. | Verify the PWM load envelope, effective capacitance and converter/limiter transient response. Model the first pixel's VIH, power-off leakage and host-report continuity during an LED overload. Record legend brightness as unmeasured. [OPSCO LED electrical characteristics](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201) |
| A physical selector can change destinations while modifiers are down, or while the new host is unreachable. | The operating-mode table addresses this with explicit routing, neutral reports and held-key suppression. Keep the same rules for wake, disconnect and BLE reconnect. Digital test cases should include a held modifier through each transition. |

The [architecture](architecture.md) assigns USB D+/D− solely to the module and keeps battery charging independent of the transport switch. Both assignments survive the switch to a CC detector. The [power budget](power-budget.md) separates source, system and cell currents and identifies the no-cell suspend path as an open design. The [pin allocation](controller-and-pins.md) preserves dedicated SWD recovery and space for the CC controls. The [keybed](keybed.md) assigns one PCB aperture per reverse-mount LED and leaves switch solder joints accessible. Before layout, check the RF keepout against the plate and battery, north-facing switch travel against the keycaps, and the stabilizer screw locations against the pack. These checks require the actual board geometry.

**Disposition for this phase.** Advance the 5 V Type-C switching-charger design to schematic capture. Carry the verified pack limits, connector current bound and RGB transient requirements into that design. Final charge time, RF behavior and lighting output remain unmeasured.

## First schematic capture

- **Connectivity.** The integrated KiCad XML export supports a repeatable pad-level check of all 67 distinct key paths, the series order of all 67 LEDs, both connector orientations for USB data, and the physical selector. The checker follows actual nets through the hierarchy. [Netlist check](../electronics/check-netlist.py)
- **LED opening.** The stock reverse-mount footprint has a nominal 3.40 × 3.00 mm opening between its straight edges. The manufacturer's maximum LED body is 3.30 × 2.90 mm. Applying a ±0.20 mm routing allowance to the stock opening gives a possible interference fit. Before board layout is frozen, dimension the minimum finished opening and corner relief, preserve pad-to-cutout clearance and lead solder overlap, and verify the resulting footprint against the intended fabrication process. Apply the resulting footprint to all 67 positions. [LED mechanical drawing](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201) · [JLCPCB fabrication capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/Capabilities?type=1)
- **RF placement.** The BMD-340 footprint includes the 68 numbered pads and an all-copper-layer antenna keepout. The board placement and case model still need to provide the physical clearance. [BMD-340 land pattern and antenna layout](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/BMD-340_DataSheet_UBX-19033353.pdf)
- **USB-C.** The captured FUSB303BTMX uses its GPIO outputs to report source current. Its table assigns H/L to detach and H/H to default-current attach. Sink Rp-change debounce is 10–20 ms. Budget the remaining response time through the actual current limiter and the load. One 150 kΩ pull-up with its output low draws about 22 µA from 3.3 V even before the detector's own current is counted. Include it in the travel-mode sleep budget. [FUSB303B tables 1, 7 and 8](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/fusb303b-d.pdf)

**Integrated electrical check.** KiCad 10.0.8 exported seven schematic pages and the complete XML netlist. The connectivity check passed for the key paths, lighting chain, USB data and selector, CC reporting, charger control bus, pack polarity, NTC and 3.3 V supply. Electrical rules report six errors and zero warnings. Four labels await the two missing power circuits (VBUS_LIMITED, HOST_500_OK, RGB_EN and LED_5V). The charger input and LED supply account for the two undriven power-pin errors. Charging is physically inhibited in this capture. [Schematic PDF](../electronics/keyboard-schematic.pdf) · [Netlist checks](../electronics/check-netlist.py)

**Assembly and placement.** Include the USB-C receptacle’s shield stakes in the JLCPCB assembly quote. Add the BMD-340 package envelope to the FreeCAD fit check because the custom footprint has no attached 3D model. Place the connector, radio, switches, stabilizers, plate and pack together before routing. Reserve clearance at the antenna and below hand-soldered pins.

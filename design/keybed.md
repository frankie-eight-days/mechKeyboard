# 67-key ANSI keybed and RGB chain

Proposed layout for the first schematic. The user selected US ANSI, soldered switches and illuminated legends. The key count, navigation legends, bottom-row gap, reference switch and stabilizer style are design choices for review.

## Switch locations and matrix

Use 19.05 mm pitch (one unit, u). In top view, the origin is the upper left of the key pitch envelope, x increases right and y increases toward the typist. Rows are 1u tall. Each name in the table occupies 1u unless a width follows in parentheses. Columns within each row are assigned in the listed order, starting at C0. The bottom-row gap consumes physical width only. This row-first numbering keeps the mapping independent of stagger. A few traces will cross between physical columns. [CHERRY MX technical data for key spacing](https://www.cherry.de/fileadmin/media/Industrial/Switch/MX_CLEAR/EN_CHERRY_MX_CLEAR.pdf)

| Matrix row | Key centers, ordered from left to right | Used columns | Switch references | Width |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R0 | Esc, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 0, Minus, Equal, Backspace (2u), Delete | C0–C14 | SW1–15 | 16u |
| R1 | Tab (1.5u), Q, W, E, R, T, Y, U, I, O, P, Left bracket, Right bracket, Backslash (1.5u), Home | C0–C14 | SW16–30 | 16u |
| R2 | Caps Lock (1.75u), A, S, D, F, G, H, J, K, L, Semicolon, Quote, Enter (2.25u), Page Up | C0–C13 | SW31–44 | 16u |

The occupied pitch envelope is 304.80 × 95.25 mm. For any key, x = (preceding widths + half its own width) × 19.05 mm, including the gap where present. y = (row index + 0.5) × 19.05 mm. Examples in millimeters include Esc (9.525, 9.525), Space (130.969, 85.725), Up (276.225, 66.675), Down (276.225, 85.725). Carry full precision into CAD. The project MX footprints, derived from the stock KiCad MX library, anchor on contact 1. On the unrotated 1u footprint, the stem is at local (−2.54, +5.08) mm. Apply the rotated stem-to-origin offset when placing the footprint from the key-center table. Align the LED aperture and plate opening to that transformed stem. The large-key footprints already contain stabilizer drills. Compare those drills to the final stabilizer drawing before adding any holes. The 16u width constrains printable case sections. The USB plug, battery bay, controller area and case walls need separate clearance envelopes.

There are 67 unique positions and 8 unused cells in a 75-cell matrix. R2/C14, R3/C14 and R4/C9–C14 have no switch. Provide grave/tilde on Fn+Esc and F1–F12 on the Fn layer. Right Shift is 1.75u, the spacebar is 6.25u and all five bottom-row modifiers are 1.25u. A sculpted keycap set also needs correctly profiled 1u navigation caps for the specified rows.

## Matrix circuit and assembly

At each occupied intersection route column → normally-open switch → diode anode. Tie the diode cathode to the row. Number each diode D1–D67 to match its switch. The tentative part is the 1N4148W, SOD-123, JLC C2099, one per switch. Show a cathode mark on silkscreen and verify it against the symbol pin number, pad number and assembly rotation. All diodes are SMT assembled. [JLCPCB 1N4148W](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/1N4148W/C2099)

Use an active-high column scan with five pulled-down row inputs. A pressed switch sources the selected column into its row through one diode. In BLE key-wake sleep, arm the five row inputs and hold all columns high. In USB travel mode, disable key wake and put the columns low. A held key otherwise establishes a path through the row pull-down and changes the sleep budget. The controller owner should verify the row high-level margin at minimum logic voltage and cold diode drop, the drive strength with five active rows, and GPIO wake configuration in the chosen sleep state. The ZMK matrix definition uses diode direction `col2row`, active-high column outputs, and active-high row inputs with pull-down. [ZMK matrix configuration](https://zmk.dev/docs/config/kscan)

Use CHERRY MX2A RGB Red five-pin **MX2A-L1NB** as the mechanical reference. It has two solder contacts, a center post, two locating pegs and an opening for an SMD LED. Keep the switch off the factory assembly BOM/CPL and include 67 in the builder's BOM. Locate the LED window toward the top of the keyboard for a top-legend keycap. Model full 4 mm travel against the chosen cap profile and switch housing before accepting the orientation. [CHERRY MX2A RGB Red datasheet](https://www.cherry.de/fileadmin/media/Industrial/Switch/MX_RED/Data_sheet_MX2A_Red.pdf)

Provide three nominal 2u stabilizers (Backspace, Enter and Left Shift) and one 6.25u spacebar stabilizer. Reserve PCB-mounted stabilizer holes and wire sweeps against the specific stabilizer drawing once selected. Fit stabilizers to the populated PCB, install the plate and align switches, then solder their two contacts from underneath. Preserve access around those joints after installing the SMT parts. The FreeCAD stackup must include the PCB, plate clips, stabilizer wire and screws, compressed keycaps, bottom-side components, battery and case bosses.

## RGB circuit and optical path

Place 67 OPSCO SK6812MINI-E reverse-mount RGB LEDs, **C5149201**, on the underside. Each shines through a routed aperture into its switch window. The JLC listing specifies Standard SMT assembly. Verify the routed aperture, pad span, pin-1 corner, bottom-side CPL rotation and emitter height against the OPSCO drawing. Pin 1 is GND, 2 DIN, 3 VDD and 4 DOUT in the linked drawing. The first LED gets level-shifted data, subsequent DIN pins connect to the preceding DOUT. Add a labeled probe pad on the final DOUT. [OPSCO SK6812MINI-E datasheet, pp. 3–8](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201) · [JLCPCB LED assembly listing](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C5149201)

Place LED1–LED67 under correspondingly numbered switches. Use a serpentine data path to keep row-to-row connections short. Chain order is **1→15, 30→16, 31→44, 58→45, 59→67**, inclusive. Firmware's first pixel is Esc and last pixel is Right. Spatial zones can use the same center coordinates as CAD.

Use a switched 5 V rail and a logic translator with a guaranteed 3.3 V input threshold at 5 V output supply. Require power-off isolation from LED DIN and power up with the first DIN pulled low. Put one tentative 100 nF ceramic at each LED, distributed bulk capacitance sized by the power owner, and a series-resistor position at the chain input. Check its value and rise time against the actual net capacitance and timing budget. Account for at least 6.7 µF of local bypass during rail startup and for synchronized PWM current pulses when dimensioning the supply. [OPSCO application circuit and electrical characteristics](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201)

Use nRF52840 SPIM2 at 8 MHz with EasyDMA, MOSI on P0.24 (U100 pad 42). Each GRB frame contains 24 bits per LED, most significant bit first. Encode a zero as `1110000000` and a one as `1111111000`, packed across byte boundaries without padding. At 125 ns per SPI bit the resulting high/low times are 0.375/0.875 µs and 0.875/0.375 µs. Both fit the OPSCO limits on page 7. A continuous RAM buffer holds 2,010 payload bytes and 300 trailing zero bytes. One DMA transfer takes 2.310 ms including the 300 µs reset. At 60 frames/s, it occupies 13.9% of the frame period. Reserve this SPIM instance for lighting, keep MOSI low when idle, and select an unused SCK GPIO if required by the driver. The firmware implementation is pending. [Nordic SPIM and EasyDMA](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/spim.html) · [OPSCO timing table](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201)

U300 drives the first DIN at the switched LED supply voltage. R301 (33 Ω) is in series at the driver to damp edge ringing. R302 (100 kΩ) pulls DIN down when U300 is high impedance. At 5 V the pulldown draws about 50 µA, dropping about 1.65 mV across R301. The 33 Ω value is provisional until the routed trace length, impedance and receiver capacitance are known. [Translator datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1t45.pdf)

After rail enable, hold the data line low through power stabilization and reset, then send the full brightness-limited frame. Before shutdown, drive data low and turn the rail off. Use the LED supply's 150 mA qualified-USB and 100 mA battery budgets. Cap each channel at code 178/255 when all three colors of that pixel are used. Apply both the per-pixel and whole-frame limits after animation and color correction. [Operating policy](rgb-power.md) [OPSCO timing and wire format](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201)

The digital release review should match every matrix cell and LED index to the netlist, overlay the actual switch/LED drawings in top view and section, inspect both sides of the board in the assembly viewer, and check the assembly BOM count (67 LEDs and 67 matrix diodes) against the placement export. Actual legend brightness, uniformity and mechanical feel remain unmeasured.

# Battery charge control

The CU-J450-V3 harness plugs into J600 with positive on pin 1, the cell-mounted thermistor on pin 2 and return on pin 3. BatterySpace identifies the fitted sensor and links its resistance table. Its R25 is 10 kΩ ±3% and its B25/85 is 3450 K ±1%. The finished pack has a 300 mA standard and 1.40 A maximum charge rating. The cell specification allows CC/CV charging from 0–45 °C and describes a 0.05 C termination condition. ## Hardware interlock

U600's REGN LDO powers the thermistor network and U601 whenever the charger's adapter stage is active. This deliberately uses a continuous reference so the independent comparator can decide whether to permit charging before CE is low. The charger's switched TS_BIAS output is unused. The charger still measures the thermistor at TS. REGN powers down on battery alone with OTG off. Count the divider current whenever REGN is on, especially in the USB suspend calculation. At REGN = 4.6 V and 25 °C the NTC divider, reference ladder and window gate together draw approximately 0.41 mA with TEMP_OK high. When it is clamped low, add approximately 46 µA. [BQ25622 §§8.3.2, 8.3.3.1 and 8.3.5.4.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

R602 is 5.23 kΩ above TS. R603 is 30.1 kΩ in parallel with the thermistor below TS. Normalized to REGN, TS is 1 / (1 + R602/RNTC + R602/R603). U601 compares this to two levels made by a separate 30.1 kΩ, 20.0 kΩ and 49.9 kΩ series string. An open-drain comparator pulls TEMP_OK low when the sensor voltage goes above the upper level or below the lower level. The 100 kΩ/100 nF gate charges slowly at turn-on and the comparator discharges it directly on fault. U600 CE can be pulled low only while both Q600 (temperature) and Q601 (positive MCU charge request) conduct. A disconnected or reset MCU gives a low request. CE has a separate default-high bias from VBUS_LIMITED. The external window operates through PMIC register and MCU resets. [Charger sheet](../electronics/charger.kicad_sch) · [TI comparator electrical limits](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv7042.pdf)

| Condition | Modeled TS / REGN | Result |
|---|---:|---|
| Thermistor short | 0% | Hot comparator inhibits |
| 45 °C, sensor and 1% divider corners | 43.67–46.17% | Inhibited |
| 25 °C, same corners | 58.08–59.77% | Window permits MCU request |
| 0 °C, same corners | 72.25–74.00% | Inhibited |
| Thermistor open | About 85% | Cold comparator inhibits |

The independent 1% reference resistors give a low threshold of 49.40–50.40% and a high threshold of 69.48–70.32%. A conservative extra 33 mV allowance for comparator offset and hysteresis at a 3.2 V operating reference adds about 1.04 percentage points either way. The two ends of the cell's permissible range still inhibit at the calculated corners. The nominal charging window is roughly 7–38 °C. The sensor table includes component production tolerance. Pack-to-sensor thermal lag, reference-rail ramps, comparator common-mode and board leakage need circuit-level validation. ## Charge profile for the firmware pass

Hold CHG_REQUEST low while discovering the source and pack. Program the charger, read back its registers and check fault/temperature status before raising the request. Use 4.18 V CV, 80 mA nominal termination, no top-off, OTG off and TS_IGNORE clear. Preserve the charger's precharge, safety timer, hardware gating and adapter current regulation. A low pack may require precharge. Inhibit on disconnect, missing pack, invalid readings, suspended default USB, or failed settings verification. Program an input ceiling below the qualified connector budget, accounting for other loads, converter tolerance and losses. A frozen requester cannot raise the USB limiter's independently qualified ceiling. Actual peripheral sequencing will be reviewed with the firmware. [BQ25622 electrical limits and register map](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

Use **1.04 A** as the high-current design setting. TI publishes ±5.5% at this setting for two stated cell voltages over –10 to 85 °C, giving 1.097 A at the upper characterized corner. This leaves 0.303 A below the finished pack maximum. Model 0.24 A for a lower-charge setting where the input budget allows it. A 4.18 V setting with +0.4% regulation error gives 4.197 V. Treat termination as a range around the programmed 80 mA and document its effect on delivered capacity. With lights off the high-current setting requires about 1.05 A at 4.75 V input, assuming 0.12 W system power and 90% conversion efficiency. With a 150 mA, 5 V LED load at 85% conversion efficiency it needs about 1.25 A. The input limiter's minimum deliverable current and the source determine which settings are usable. [TI charge accuracy and termination](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

Dividing 1.50 Ah by 1.04 A gives **87 minutes before allowance for taper** at nominal capacity. Two hours from normal discharge cutoff requires at least 0.75 A average cell current. The supplier's standard-charge test and partial charge plot do not give the full duration at our chosen CC, CV and termination settings. Use a 5 V source advertising 3 A for the two-hour target. The conservative 1.5 A input tier requires a lower programmed charge current. [Supplier charge plot](https://www.batteryspace.com/prod-specs/4312-703562-3.7V1500mAh.jpg)

# Power and mechanical fit review

## Battery path

The exported charger netlist connects J600 pin 2 to both U601 comparison channels and U600 TS. U601 clamps the Q600 gate when TS crosses either threshold. Q600 and the MCU-request transistor Q601 sit in series between charger CE and ground. The 100 kΩ pull-down at Q601 makes a floating MCU charge request inactive. The TS network and reference ladder share REGN. [Charge control and pad assignments](charge-control.md) · [TI comparator](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv7042.pdf)

Sweeping the pack manufacturer's sensor limits and independent 1% resistor corners gives 72.25–74.00% of REGN for TS at 0 °C, 58.08–59.77% at 25 °C and 43.67–46.17% at 45 °C. Comparator threshold resistor corners span 69.48–70.32% and 49.40–50.40%. Including 33 mV of input offset and hysteresis at 3.2 V leaves the 0 °C and 45 °C endpoint voltages outside the charging window. The 1.04 A configuration has 303 mA margin to the assembled pack maximum at TI's published positive current error. [Sensor tolerance table](https://www.batteryspace.com/prod-specs/3602.pdf) · [Charger limits](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

At 1.04 A, transferring 1.50 Ah takes at least 86.5 minutes before taper. As an illustrative two-hour scenario, an exponential taper from 1.04 A to 80 mA averages 0.374 A during the taper. Under that assumption the CC stage must deliver about 1.17 Ah before taper begins. Runtime, thermal/current regulation, the actual taper, and the early termination associated with 4.18 V CV determine delivered capacity. TI reports that the low-current termination comparator can terminate 10–20 mA above the programmed value. [Battery cell test conditions](https://www.batteryspace.com/prod-specs/4312-703562.pdf) · [TI §8.3.5.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

## Lighting and fit

The LED chain and physical fit map agree on 67 positions. Following the stipulated 180° MX rotation, FreeCAD models the two large-key stabilizer hole Y offsets at −7.00 and +8.24 mm in its rear-positive Y frame. Their center distances are ±11.90 mm for Backspace, Enter and left Shift and ±50.00 mm for Space, matching the exported KiCad footprints. The keycap and switch recess remain purchasing envelopes. Optical output and interference with an actual keycap profile require the chosen vendor's geometry or measurement. [Fit model generator](../mechanical/build_fit.py) · [Positions](../mechanical/key-centers.csv)

The 150 mA USB and 100 mA battery budget applies to the whole LED bank. Add the supplier's per-package grayscale condition to firmware. For any tricolor pixel, clamp each channel to code 178/255 before evaluating the global budget. One pixel at full white would pass the global 150 mA test on its own. The sparse fallback also needs the per-pixel clamp. [OPSCO application conditions, p. 3](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201) · [Lighting model](rgb-power.md)

The worst-case charge excursion for a static 150 mA frame at 1 kHz and 752 µF is 200 mV before ESR and trace resistance. Add about 38 mV for the initial lumped ESR and 30 mV for a 10 mΩ loop at the estimated simultaneous peak. Recheck the distributed paths in the layout. At 20 °C, a 110 mm run in 35 µm copper with equal-width supply and return needs approximately 11 mm of width per conductor for a 10 mΩ total. Local ground planes, necks, vias and the second bulk capacitor change the real result. Evaluate fast load release as well as sag, and verify that the converter remains stable with the selected bank. [TI TPS61252 §11.2.2.7](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf) · [Panasonic bulk capacitor](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/poscap/models/6TPE470M)

## USB source limits and battery-free operation

The input switch has a 19.2 kΩ base resistance. The decoder selects an additional 634 Ω or 1.24 kΩ for a 3 A or 1.5 A advertisement. Effective resistances of 614 Ω and 1.165 kΩ lie close to the 610 Ω and 1.15 kΩ characterization points. From the supplier limits of 1.54–2.46 A and 0.75–1.25 A at those points, the upper figures leave connector margin, and the lower 1.5 A tier can deliver less than the roughly 1.05 A input needed for the proposed 1.04 A charge setting even with lights off. Use the 3 A advertisement for the fast-charge target and derate the 1.5 A case. The 4.32 kΩ legacy branch produces 3.53 kΩ effective. Its operating tolerance requires characterization or a manufacturer bound between specified points. [TI TPS22950-Q1 §§6.5 and 8.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22950-q1.pdf)

With no cell, disconnecting the main charger input during host suspend makes the BQ25622 repeat its 220 ms qualification delay on resume. The review added D801 to power logic from the limited USB path as soon as U800 switches on. D800 independently retains the battery/SYS path. R801 bounds the separate retention feed. At 4.40 V VBUS, assuming a 0.30 V diode drop, its theoretical maximum deliverable power is about 1.27 mW. At a modeled 80% converter efficiency this gives about 0.31 mA available at 3.3 V. Verify the pre-enumeration, suspend and resume firmware states against that budget and verify the rail hold-up through U800 turn-on. Include the transceiver and pull-up on their actual supply domains. [TI charger startup sequence](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf) · [USB limiter schematic](../electronics/usb-limit.kicad_sch)

First attach needs its own power-state case. As soon as an unconfigured nRF52840 asserts its D+ pull-up, the bus can stay in its idle J state through host connection debounce. During that interval the logic must keep attachment valid with no cell and with the main switch inactive, or retain a bounded startup path until the first bus activity. Ordinary awake boot current cannot be assumed to fit the retention allocation. An instrumented firmware power model should cover the first attach, a host that never begins enumeration, and a host resume with the cell disconnected. [USB 2.0 specification, Chapters 7 and 9](https://www.usb.org/document-library/usb-20-specification) · [USB limiter schematic](../electronics/usb-limit.kicad_sch)

The integrated revision connects the D+ pull-up edge through R817 to U803 pin 2. This starts a finite window for the MCU to enter sleep even when the first attach is followed by a long idle interval. The bus state now gates the MCU suspend request so a stuck request can release on resume. The timing and load budgets in the [USB design](usb-limit.md) still require verification with the firmware build.

# Switched RGB supply

U900 supplies a separate nominal 4.99 V rail from SYS. Its internal switches disconnect the load in shutdown. U901 monitors SYS and can turn the lighting off independently of the microcontroller. The 3.3 V keyboard regulator takes its input upstream of U900. Keep its supply and return away from high-current LED traces. [Editable RGB power sheet](../electronics/rgb-power.kicad_sch) · [TI TPS61252, §§10–11](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf) · [TI TPS3808](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf)

## Electrical limits

R901/R902 set VOUT to 1.2 × (1 + 768/243) = 4.993 V. With independent 1% resistor errors and 1.182–1.218 V reference limits, the range is 4.844–5.145 V before line/load variation, transient ripple and temperature coefficients. The SK6812MINI-E-012 operating range is 3.7–5.5 V. The 100 pF feed-forward capacitor parallels R901. Two 22 µF, 10 V ceramics sit at VOUT. TI requires at least 8 µF effective there. An identical 22 µF ceramic decouples VIN. [TI design procedure](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf) · [Murata GRM31CR71A226KE15](https://pim.murata.com/en-us/pim/details/?partNum=GRM31CR71A226KE15%23) · [OPSCO characteristics, p. 6](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201)

R900 is 20 kΩ, the exact setting at which TI specifies ±20% valley current tolerance. Including its 1% resistance tolerance gives a 396–606 mA programmed valley. As a load example, use 82% efficiency, 2.2 µH at −20% and 3.25 MHz typical switching. At SYS = 3.2 V the ripple is about 274 mA peak-to-peak, upper peak about 880 mA and upper cycle average about 743 mA. These figures depend on actual inductance under bias and switching frequency. L900 has a 2.4 A saturation rating. TI's simplified output-current formula gives about 202 mA at the low current-limit corner, 3.2 V input and 5.145 V output. At 3.02 V the estimate is 190 mA. Reserve margin with a **150 mA normal USB rail allocation**, with 200 mA retained as an engineering envelope, and 100 mA on battery. [TPS61252 §§8.5, 10.3.2 and 11.2.2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf) · [Murata inductor specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/9a2227c0960d033e774a76ebdabff58d.pdf)

**Startup and source policy.** Keep the branch off during blank-MCU boot, unqualified attach, no-cell default USB and USB suspend when the source lacks a higher-current grant. Enable from a healthy battery or a qualified source with sufficient headroom after core and charge demand. For uninterrupted startup from a 1.5 A source, reserve 0.9 A at SYS for the lighting stage, first reducing charge demand. An absent-battery 500 mA host has insufficient margin for this startup policy. The firmware should read pack and power-path status before relying on battery supplement under a lesser USB grant. On a source downgrade, clear the lighting request or establish the new power budget. The connector limit and charger input regulation remain independently responsible for their source allocation. [TI boost soft start](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf) · [Charger power path](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

## Synchronized pixels

The 67 LEDs have 201 channels. At 25 °C the manufacturer gives 14.5 mA maximum per color, 0.6 mA typical idle current per pixel and 1 kHz typical PWM. It supplies no minimum PWM frequency or maximum idle current. Reserve 1 mA idle per LED and 2 mA for the two bulk capacitors' leakage, bleeder, divider and bank overhead. The model's full-white fault peak is approximately 2.98 A. For each *final frame*, including effects and brightness correction, constrain 69 + 14.5 × Σ(code/255) in mA. The default 150 mA USB setting leaves 81 mA for emission, equivalent to all-white code 7/255 after rounding down. The 100 mA battery setting gives code 2/255. The cap also applies to startup and diagnostics. These levels favor color or localized patterns. Legend visibility requires optical measurement. [OPSCO data, pp. 3 and 6](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201)

C904 and C905 each provide 470 µF, ±20%, 25 mΩ maximum ESR at 100 kHz and 20 °C. Their parallel initial lower-bound capacitance is 752 µF. At 1 kHz and a static 150 mA frame the total charge drawn over one whole PWM cycle is 150 µC, including fully aligned channels. Even assigning that entire cycle to the capacitor bank, the modeled excursion is 200 mV + 38 mV lumped ESR. A 10 mΩ end-to-end supply-and-return route adds 30 mV at 3 A. At the 200 mA envelope these terms sum to 334 mV, leaving about 29 mV before the most sensitive PG threshold (7.5% below a 4.844 V starting point). Allowing a further 20% loss of capacitance and a 1.5× ESR increase under Panasonic's endurance criteria, the 150 mA result becomes about 335 mV including routing. These are per-cycle, steady-frame estimates with zero converter contribution during the pulse and adequate recharge between pulses. The result depends on layout inductance, branch current sharing, PWM distribution and actual regulator loop response. A 500 Hz static pattern doubles the capacitive droop term. Latching a new frame can also bunch adjacent pulses. [Panasonic 6TPE470M characteristics](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/poscap/models/6TPE470M) · [TPE endurance criteria](https://industrial.panasonic.com/content/data/CP/PDF/POSCAP/S_POSCAP_TPE_E.pdf)

Use a separate firmware limit for rapid pattern changes: permit at most five nonzero channels on USB or two on battery in a conservative sparse mode. That gives upper instantaneous estimates of 141.5 and 98 mA even for fully aligned PWM, conditional on the 69 mA overhead and color-current allocations. This mode supports indicators and localized RGB while the normal full-keybed mode uses the bulk storage. On power-good failure shut the branch down. Resume a sparse scene after deliberate retry and a new source/pack check. Continuous rail stability, electrical emissions and optical output across board layout and production variation remain unverified.

## Supervision and fault behavior

U901 uses its factory 3.07 V threshold on SYS, with CT open and MR tied to SYS. Threshold accuracy is ±1.5% over the full temperature range. Nominal recovery waits 20 ms (12–28 ms). The supervisor's open-drain reset can clamp U900 EN while the MCU's request stays high. R904 isolates the GPIO, R905 defaults the gate low, and R906 pulls PG up to this *gated enable*. MCU pad 57 (P1.01) reads PG with its internal pull disabled. With EN low the TPS61252 releases PG, and the external network biases it low. With lighting off, budget 3.5 µA maximum U900 shutdown draw and up to 6 µA U901 VDD draw at the cited 6.5 V test condition. The fixed SENSE pin also draws about 1.7 µA typical at 6.5 V; TI gives no worst-case figure for that contribution. Include these terms in the whole-keyboard travel budget. U901 can restart the converter after voltage recovery if firmware stalls with a high request. The clamp covers sustained undervoltage. The datasheet gives a typical 20 µs SENSE reaction at 5% overdrive. A weak upstream source, short fast dips or another concurrent load can still disturb logic. Simulate those on the routed power network before claiming continuous HID during a fault. [TPS3808, §§6.5–6.6](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf) · [TPS61252, §10.3.6](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf)

Hold RGB_DATA low during startup. The bank stores about 12 mJ nominal at 5 V, plus the ceramic capacitors. Charging it from a 3.2 V, 396 mA input with 82% conversion efficiency would take at least about 12 ms neglecting pixel current. Allow up to 250 ms for PG to assert and stabilize. On timeout deassert RGB_EN and continue HID. Send an all-black frame before the first requested scene. After startup, a falling PG triggers immediate software shutdown and clears the request. Avoid automatic repeated retries. Before normal power-off send black, wait for the frame latch, drive DIN low and deassert EN. A 10 kΩ bleeder provides a permanent discharge path (about 0.5 mA at 5 V). Bulk discharge can take tens of seconds. If a full discharge is required after a fault, use 75 s at room temperature as a policy allowance based on approximately 1.2 mF and 10 kΩ. Residual voltage and leakage need verification in board layout.

A hard short can discharge the bulk capacitors directly into the fault. Subsequent energy comes through U900's current-limited power stage and thermal protection. The 20 kΩ setting limits sustained converter input. Copper, connectors and capacitor banks still see pulse current. Allocate the two 7.3 × 4.3 × 3.8 mm capacitors along the low-impedance LED path. Keep them clear of the battery and switch pins. Pad 1 is positive. The local footprint uses two 2.4 × 2.9 mm lands separated by 3.7 mm per Panasonic D4. Use the USB input limiter for upstream USB protection. [Panasonic land drawing](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/AAA8000/ast-ind-139507.pdf) · [TPS61252 thermal and overload behavior](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf)

## Sourcing and CAD checks

The 17 SMT placements carry orderable MPNs and LCSC codes. LCSC showed 2,305 of the TPS61252DSGR, 50 of the TPS3808G33DBVR and 25 of the 6TPE470M on 17 November 2026. Two bulk capacitors per board limit that snapshot to 12 boards before attrition. JLCPCB listed the supervisor in overseas inventory. Confirm all allocations and two-sided SMT setup with the assembly order. [Boost C128617](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C128617.html) · [Supervisor C43698](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C43698.html) · [Bulk capacitor C427179](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C427179.html)

KiCad exported the child sheet to PDF and XML. The XML connects SYS to U900 pin 8 and L900 pin 1, L900 pin 2 to U900 SW pin 7, VOUT pin 2 to both bulk positive pads, FB pin 3 to the divider and 100 pF capacitor, and pad 9 to ground. U901 pins 3, 5 and 6 connect to SYS, CT pin 4 is intentionally open, and its output pin 1 joins the EN gate. Reduced loop-gain margin with roughly 1 mF of distributed storage and light-load PFM response remain layout-review items. The converter data provide a minimum operating capacitance and typical application waveforms rather than a stability envelope for this exact bank.

For every frame, including sparse fault recovery and diagnostics, limit each channel of a tricolor pixel to a maximum code of 178/255 (70% grayscale) before applying the total current budget.

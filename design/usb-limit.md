# USB source-current limiter and suspend supply

U800 is a TPS22950CQDDCRQ1 between protected USB_VBUS and VBUS_LIMITED. It supplies the BQ25622 input and an independent diode path to the logic converter. USB_VBUS also supplies the radio’s dedicated USB PHY and a resistance-limited retention branch. U800’s ON pin rises through R800 at power-up. N-channel switches can pull it low for suspend. Pin 4 always sees R802 and R803 in series to ground. Their combined 19.2 kΩ establishes the cold-start limit with no MCU program, no pack and no 3.3 V logic. C800 is a 10 µF, 50 V ceramic at VIN. Its typical effective capacitance at 5 V is 7.28 µF. Keep all these paths at 5 V. ## Connector grants

U801 decodes the two FUSB303B GPIOs. U802 inhibits decoder outputs until the 3.3 V supply passes its 2.93 V supervisor threshold and its 140–460 ms reset interval expires. During this interval the keyboard can enumerate at its cold-start budget. Controller startup should wait for the power rails and USB recovery hardware to settle before enabling the full-speed pull-up. U800 remains available throughout startup and host reset unless firmware expressly forces suspend. Each Type-C FET gate has a 100 kΩ pull-down, a 10 kΩ/100 nF turn-on filter and a diode across the charge resistor to accelerate turn-off. The default host branch has a separate 100 kΩ gate pull-down. ## USB host, suspend and resume

U803 contains two retriggerable timers. The first samples rising D+ and falling D− transitions through two 1 kΩ taps and asserts BUS_ACTIVE. The initial pull-up on D+ starts a full timer interval before the first SOF. The second samples rising edges on HOST_500_OK and asserts HOST_ALIVE. Only an active default-current attach with both outputs high receives the 306 mA tier. Firmware must request 500 mA in the USB configuration descriptor and generate a short GPIO pulse from each **actual, configured** full-speed SOF callback. Stop immediately on deconfiguration, bus reset, detach or suspend. The bus timer has 100 kΩ and 100 nF and the host-grant timer uses 39 kΩ and 100 nF. Using the vendor’s typical 2–5 V coefficient range, a 1% resistor and a provisional 80–120 nF effective capacitor gives a **3.56–6.67 ms** bus window and **1.39–2.60 ms** grant window. The chip coefficient is typical, so these are design windows pending corner verification. A stuck high or low MCU output expires. Unconfigured traffic can refresh only the first timer. On USB reset the default tier returns after the heartbeat expires. [Nexperia 74HC123, §10 note 2](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT123.pdf)

With CC_OUT1 high, a quiet bus and D+ high, Q805–Q807 pull U800 ON low independently of the CPU. U800 disconnects the charger and discharges VBUS_LIMITED. Q808 extends this same idle override to higher Type-C tiers when USB_SUSPEND is high. Q805 and Q806 are in series with both cutoff paths. A USB K/resume drives D+ low and live packet traffic pulls BUS_QUIET low, so even a stuck-high USB_SUSPEND cannot prevent electrical restart. Clear the GPIO in firmware on resume. Keep the two 1 kΩ timer taps and the weak 100 kΩ D+ sense close to the data pair, with no test-pad branches. Validate full-speed waveform loading at both D lines. Establish pulse width limits under process/temperature and capacitor bias, then check timeout against 1 ms packets and the USB suspend deadline. The timers monitor electrical traffic. Firmware supplies USB configuration state.

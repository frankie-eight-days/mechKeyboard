# Rev F electrical audit before PCB layout

**Hold electrical sign-off for layout.** Three Sol reviewers audited the populated Rev F design by component and signal. They then independently challenged one another’s limits and calculations. The source schematic and component selections were left at the reviewed revision. [Filterable workbook](electrical-audit.xlsx) · [Original 19-item register](../prelayout-review.md)

The workbook has a **Case verdicts** tab. Its scope column matters. A passing capacitor rating comparison says the specified working or screen voltage is below the part rating. Voltage withstand, effective capacitance, lifetime, and converter stability are separate claims. A negative logic margin derived using incompatible test currents is insufficient evidence for a pass and does not demonstrate malfunction. ## Decisions, ordered by when a circuit change may be needed

Numbers in parentheses identify affected items in the earlier register. The first three decisions remain the battery floor, powered USB protection and battery-free USB retention. Rows below record the new component-level evidence and the evidence needed for closure.

| Circuit and owner | What the audit established | Closure and gate |
|---|---|---|
| **Pack cutoff and charger state** · Battery/firmware (01, 12, 13) | The normal cell floor is 2.75 V versus a 2.40 ±0.10 V PCM trip. R600 sets ~100 mA until the external-limit override is deliberately changed. The independent temperature calculation now shows positive margins at both forbidden-boundary screens, including +44 mV at 0 °C. Valid NTC with BAT+ open is still possible. | **Before power placement**, establish independent cutoff, hysteresis, drain and restart. Freeze source-qualified charger register programming. Document elapsed charge time as an estimate unless CC/CV and thermal evidence become available. |
| **Live USB OV and front-end UV** · Power (02, 09) | Reproduced 5.824 V on the 5.80 V absolute-maximum PHY in the specified 6 V live source fault. New divider corner gives 5.510–5.792 V rising OV and 3.699–3.972 V falling UV. The low OV trip has only 10 mV above 5.50 V in that sensitivity. Some units may need USB to fall below 5.50 V to re-enable after an OV trip. The two 25 V raw capacitors and 30 V pass FETs further limit port-fault claims. | Define amplitude, source impedance, rise, duration and recovery. Qualify or change sensing/clamping/PHY feed before placement. Inspect power-up, held-high, source bounce and load release, including fault duration and regulator input stress. |
| **USB-only retention and source grants** · Power/USB (03, 09, 14) | One R801 path screen provides at most 0.285 mA at 3.3 V. FUSB GPIO-mode OUT1/OUT2 are open-drain. The nearby I²C-mode leakage/sink figures do not establish the complete logic margin of 150 kΩ status pulls and Q807 over temperature. | Bound total upward/downward leakage, HC thresholds, stuck-state and grant/downgrade timing. Show retention and connector suspend current simultaneously, or change the power architecture. Close detached 50 µA budget separately. |
| **USB timing returns and decoder outputs** · USB logic (03, 09, 10) | **New schematic discrepancy.** U803 CEXT pins 6 and 14 go only to their individual capacitors. Nexperia Figure 11 recommends an external connection to U803 ground pin 8 for noise immunity. Typical RC timing is not a guaranteed timeout. D802/D803 also discharge gate capacitors into U801 outputs around the charging resistor. No external resistance establishes a bounded peak at the 25 mA per-output rating. | Resolve CEXT grounding on the schematic and calculate timing over component and IC variation. Bound peak output current for a charged capacitor and confirm allowed grant-release timing if series resistance is added. [Nexperia 74HC123 Figure 11](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT123.pdf) |
| **Logic source OR** · Power (04) | D800/D801 normal reverse voltage is comfortably below rectifier rating. D800 battery insertion current and unequal D801 current sharing remain unbounded. U700's recommended maximum is 5.5 V. At an upstream 5.760 V release, protection requires at least 260 mV attenuation or path isolation before its VIN. No guaranteed minimum Schottky drop proves that. | Calculate the time waveform at LOGIC_IN on battery insertion, cutoff, USB takeover and repeated pulses. Qualify current, junction temperature and overvoltage or change the parts. |
| **OLED power and bus** · Controls (05, 11) | Tied panel VDD/VBAT have only a single boundary value in the bare-panel recommended ranges. At the screened 3.231 V module input an ideal series regulator already misses the VBAT minimum by 69 mV. The connector-side 1 µs I²C rise-time check also does not establish the panel's 300 ns edge limit or the ACK sinking required through the breakout. | Obtain guaranteed conditions for the exact assembled module or change display/power interface. Include reset option, pulldown/idle states, internal level shifter and harness. |
| **Haptic power and PMIC bus** · Power/firmware (06, 18) | No independent U1100 supply cycle. DRV-side bus uses only the translator's 7.5–12.5 kΩ internal pull-ups against TI's 660 Ω–4.7 kΩ recommendation. For MCU→charger data the raw bounds allow only 20 mV ground/noise margin at compatible drive/load. During a clean haptic ACK the charger is unselected. An illustrative negative 6 mV at the charger on that ACK is not a demonstrated lost transaction. | Provide reachable recovery, select drive and pull-ups, and budget local ground offset and bus capacitance. Preserve safe charger traffic across a stuck haptic bus. Consider local pull-up sites before layout. |
| **Matrix** · Controller (07) | All 67 routes are correct and the reverse-voltage rating of each matrix diode clears the normal screen. Five asserted rows need ~1.034 mA at the worst illustrated input threshold. The fitted diode lacks a guaranteed cold drop at the actual ~0.2 mA branch current. | Bound forward drop, column drive, reverse leakage for idle rows and scan-settle time as a single design. Use better-specified diodes or change bias if those limits cannot be established. |
| **RGB power bank and inhibit** · Power (08) | C904/C905 have **0.80 V** nameplate margin if LED_5V stays ≤5.50 V. Their stored charge discharges locally into a short, bypassing the converter input limit. At 5.50 V a simple resistive screen requires ≥0.275 Ω per cap for ≤20 A; catalog maximum ESR cannot prove minimum impedance. U901 reset VOL and U900 EN-low limit both reach **0.40 V**, giving zero catalog noise margin for the forced-off function. | Qualify or alter the bank, discharge/fault path and inhibit circuit before committing their geometry. Bound edge rates, converter loop stability, route loss and dynamic PG at the allowed animation load. Use 4.800–5.193 V as a hot divider sensitivity, before FB leakage. |
| **RGB signal chain** · Controller (08, 15) | The planned 8 MHz custom SPI encoding meets the selected timing on paper. The translator's static table lacks the fitted mixed-supply output corner, and the pixel sheet lacks guaranteed DOUT VOH/VOL. All 66 hops are present. They remain electrically unqualified at rail and ground corners. | Obtain actual interface bounds or limit the supported operating envelope. Preserve first/last data and local return access. Compile and verify concurrent firmware before claiming animation performance. |
| **USB data and RF hardware** · USB/RF (10, 17) | Reference 27 Ω USB series sites absent; two 1 kΩ timer taps and a 100 kΩ gate tap add branches. TVS ratings and all four channels were reviewed, including the difference between standoff, breakdown, clamp and ESD pulse stress. | Decide damping/tuning sites, isolate collapsing domains and allocate radio/PHY bypass. Check crystal parasitics, UICR reset, probe cable and exact incoming module revision. |
| **Assembly, routing and power loops** · Libraries/PCB (11, 16, 18, 19) | U200 pad/paste differences, J200 slot discrepancy and SW400 dry-contact evidence remain. All key drills match the mechanical transfer, with switch origin displaced from stem center. Timing capacitors use X7R with substantial tolerance over life. Seven resistors warrant pulse qualification. | Freeze manufacturer land-pattern dispositions, then implement netclasses, staged capacitor returns, return-plane continuity, RF, battery and boss keepouts. Check board DRC/CAM and actual assembler BOM/CPL when physical files exist. |
| **Firmware agreement** · Firmware (12–15, 17) | Pad 60 is HAPTIC_EN in the native netlist. Stock short-symbol SPI encoding does not implement the chosen custom waveform. Ship mode exit needs valid VBUS. | Correct the pin table during the authorized design update, pin the driver settings, and verify reset/watchdog and mode transitions. Two-hour charging, travel current and host interoperability remain open product claims. |

## Independent challenge and remaining evidence

Three cross-reviews covered the passives, logic ledger and supply rails. The audit incorporated the conditional U700 overvoltage case, corrected the TPS7A02 reverse-current interpretation, and distinguished a physical I²C voltage at an unselected device from a transaction for which it must receive data. It also added the U803 grounding discrepancy and the wider RGB divider sensitivity. [Independent challenge record](challenges.md) · [Calculations and transfer details](interactions.md)

The active project matched the review baseline SHA-256 **fc8c5f02b26380020e7fbfe725abb3f66d2b5875c6a8d9d18683cb44a8d5de5b** across 34 native design/library files. The 48-item release coverage stays recorded in the [original checklist ledger](../prelayout-review.md). This audit adds pin, component and state evidence to the power, interface, library and handoff checks. Final board DRC/CAM, PCB parasitics, a complete firmware implementation, EMC, measured charging/runtime, and assembled electrical performance remain open. No orders, hardware tests or design ECOs were made.

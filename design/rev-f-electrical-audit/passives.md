# Rev F passive component audit

Reviewed all 122 capacitors, 69 fixed resistors, RV1000 and three power inductors in the frozen Eeschema 10.0.8 Rev F export. All 195 references have matching MPN, LCSC code and physical terminal nets in the ledgers. The capacitor inventory resolves to 14 exact MPNs. The two ledgers include the dated five-board sourcing screen. Stock is a snapshot and requires confirmation at assembly order. ## How to read the electrical screens

The CSV voltage columns are magnitude across the two named terminals, including every nonground capacitor. They show calculated screens or explicitly named circuit limits. A requirement such as 5.50 V on LED_5V remains a limit to prove. No finite voltage rating bounds an unspecified external source, connector ESD or a failed regulator outside its operating conditions. Each exceptional row states its source model or limiting assumption. Absolute-maximum IC values used to size passives do not qualify the attached IC for operation at those values. The 3V3 3.45 V working screen and 3.60 V shared-domain design ceiling account separately for unknown release overshoot. The SYS 4.45 V working screen includes pack and load margin, and 5.20 V is the haptic operating ceiling to enforce in design. Use the tighter device limit for attached loads.

For X5R and X7R, multiplying ±10% initial and ±15% unbiased temperature gives a useful initial/TCC screen of 76.5–126.5% before separately evidenced voltage bias and aging. It does not establish a guaranteed product at simultaneous combined conditions. X5R parts C201, C602, C604–C607 and C800 have a +85 °C dielectric rating. X7R parts have +125 °C rating. Class II capacitance changes with AC test voltage and time after heat treatment. Samsung's exact reference sheets §A/C and handling §§2-2, 2-4 and 2-5 show the applicable measurement conditions and typical bias and aging behavior. Require an exact-part bias/TCC model at the actual rail and an explicit lifetime or lot margin. For example, adding an illustrative 10% age reserve reduces the initial/TCC floor from 76.5% to 68.85% of nameplate before bias. It requires 33.0% remaining bias retention for C700 at 5 µF and for C701/C702 at 10 µF total, 26.4% for C902/C903 at 8 µF total, and 66.0% for the C1105/C1107 pair to sustain at least 0.5 µF. The local C1105 by U1101 must meet stability impedance at the relevant frequency even when counting the line to remote C1107 separately. [Murata exact 22 µF SimSurfing](https://ds.murata.com/simsurfing/mlcc.html?oripartnumbers=%5B%22GRM31CR71A226KE15L%22%5D&partnumbers=%5B%22GRM31CR71A226KE15%22%5D&rgear=inovutkyzuv&rgearinfo=bg) · [Samsung exact 1 µF bias graph](https://product.samsungsem.com/mlcc/CL10B105KA8NFN.do) · [TI TPS7A02 §6.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a02.pdf)

The 18 pF C100/C101 load pair is Fenghua CG C0G ±1%, 50 V. Its series load is 8.91–9.09 pF before layout parasitics and temperature drift. Epson's 12.5 pF nominal load therefore needs approximately 3.4–3.6 pF addition at the crystal terminals. Pair tolerance and ±30 ppm/°C drift are small relative to parasitic spread. Verify Nordic's load/ESR/drive condition, Epson's 70 kΩ max ESR and 0.5 µW max drive, and startup with the actual board. C203 is Yageo NP0 ±5% and gives 9.12 µs nominal, 8.66–9.57 µs from C tolerance alone using 91.15 kΩ. C900 is Murata C0G ±5%; 768 kΩ puts the feed-forward zero at 2.07 kHz nominal. The USB monostable C803/C804 intentionally use X7R. Their nominal RC products are 10.0/3.90 ms before multiplication by the HC123 coefficient. Initial/temp-only C and R gives roughly 7.52–12.86 / 2.93–5.02 ms, before bias, aging, and silicon timing. C1108's nominal 10.5 ms to 0.65 VCCA grows to about 16.3 ms including 1.2 ms LDO start under the illustrative upper 126.5 nF, 101.65 kΩ and ±1 µA OE leak screen. Verify the actual charging ramp and UVLO isolation. ## Bias, ripple and ratio evidence

- **Charger/input.** C600 is 47 nF, 50 V X7R with one terminal on SW and the other on BTST. BQ25622 allows 6 V across BTST–SW and ±ground excursions separately. C601/C602/C605–606 meet TI's 1/10/20 µF nominal criteria without derating as specified in §7.3. C602 at 5 V is about 7.276 µF in Samsung's typical model. The modeled broadband ripple is 0.613 A RMS C602 and 0.152 A C603, plus a 0.716 A screen if all corner demand lands in C602. At 4.46 mΩ typical ESR this gives about 2.29 mW, before frequency/temperature and layout shifts. The three 47 µF AVX 16 V parts are 126.9 µF at minus their initial tolerance and 22.5 µA summed maximum rated-voltage leakage at 25 °C. Each is 150 mΩ max and 1.00/0.90/0.40 A RMS at 25/85/125 °C, 100 kHz. C1101 has the same limits on the motor SYS rail. Cap polarity is pad 1 positive and package polarity stripe plus; ensure no reverse drive at VBAT/SYS collapse. The AVX 10 V category rating at 125 °C and 20 V/13 V surge ratings at 85 °C/125 °C give separate voltage screens. Evaluate duration, source impedance, re-energization and individual crest current before applying them. [BQ25622 §§7.1/7.3, 9 and 11](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf) · [Charger input ripple model](../charger-input-ripple.md) · [KYOCERA AVX TPS p. 70](https://datasheets.kyocera-avx.com/TPS.pdf)
- **RGB.** C904/C905 together provide 752 µF initial low, and 602 µF if another −20% applies in the endurance screen. Their two leakage limits sum to 592.2 µA at rated voltage. The 3.0 A each RMS catalog condition is 100 kHz and 45 °C; it does not establish performance at low-frequency coincident PWM, a short, or rising application ambient. R903 bleeds a nominal ≈991 µF bank with a 9.91 s time constant. R901/R902 ratio and VFB give 4.844–5.145 V from independent initial limits before load, line, TCR and aging. R900 gives 396–606 mA valley including the characterized ±20% at 20 kΩ and initial tolerance. Treat the full bank/ESR and LED rail ripple as a separate stability and load-release test.

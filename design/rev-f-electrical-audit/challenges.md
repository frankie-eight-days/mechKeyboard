# Independent cross-checks and dispositions

| Challenged claim | Cross-check | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| C201 can use its dielectric withstand as working voltage | Exact Samsung CL21A475KAQNNNE is 4.7 µF, X5R, 25 V. A qualification withstand test is not a repetitive working rating. In the specified cold passive network its node cannot exceed the extrema of its initial voltage and driving waveform. | Keep 7.697 V as a sampled positive cold-event screen, and 17.303 V nameplate headroom for that event. Sustained raw-port faults remain open. |
| RGB bank and R901/R902 qualify a fixed 5 V | Independent check on R901/R902 opposing TCR and initial tolerance with 1.182–1.218 V FB reference gives 4.800–5.193 V at 85 °C. A hypothetical 0.1 µA FB leakage shifts output about 76.8 mV through 768 kΩ. | 4.844–5.145 V remains the initial divider/reference screen in the ledgers. Apply the wider range to hot steady-state screening. This leaves ~307 mV to a 5.50 V LED maximum before leakage and transients. The pulse and loop conditions remain open. |
| Timer values prove USB timeout and rapid discharge | C803 100 nF × 100 kΩ is 10 ms RC. Applying typical K endpoints and C initial/temperature limits gives about 3.41–7.03 ms in one screen. Nexperia Figure 11 recommends both CEXT terminals be externally grounded. Netlist shows isolated two-terminal nodes. U801 current pulse through D802/D803 has no external series limit in the discharge path. | Resolve timer return paths, noise and the setup/expiry bounds before routing. Treat GPIO pulse current independently of diode reverse rating. |
| R204 passes all faults and far-end C367 guarantees the LED voltage | Pulse-model R204 energy and peak are finite. Manufacturer pulse chart starts at 100 µs while one equivalent rectangular event is ~2.05 µs. C367 is a 50 V local capacitor, but its presence cannot establish pixel ground shift or edge voltage. | Normal DC/rating screens stand. Qualify the actual short-pulse/repetition envelope, C201 short upstream of U800, and far-end PDN/return before production. |
| U700 is protected at the 5.5 V input limit | D801 supplies LOGIC_IN while U800 conducts. Under a modeled upstream 5.760 V release, no specified minimum diode drop proves a 5.50 V ceiling on U700. | Added a conditional supply row requiring ≥260 mV attenuation or demonstrated isolation. No U700 overvoltage waveform was measured. |
| TPS7A02 reverse current guidance relaxes its absolute VOUT rating | Reviewed TI §6.1 separately from reverse-current operational guidance. | Applied the stricter of VIN+0.3 V and 5.5 V to U1101 OUT. Reverse-current guidance is additional. |
| OLED works because the completed breakout is sold for 3.3 V | Manufacturer's nominal module support is favorable evidence. It does not resolve the guaranteed assembled operating window, exact glass revision or reset suffix at the screened low VIN. | Keep as a component-interface decision, without claiming an observed blank screen. |
| Haptic ACK causes a dropped charger transaction | DRV address 0x5A differs from BQ address 0x6B. The charger sees that voltage physically while unselected in a clean haptic frame. At ~1.03 mA, keeping the charger node ≤0.42 V above a 0.40 V DRV output requires transfer resistance ≤19.6 Ω at zero offset, ~14.7 Ω at +5 mV or ~9.8 Ω at +10 mV. The 25 Ω data-sheet row is at a different test condition. The DRV SCL pin is an input. | Retain as a shared-bus fault/transition concern. During a charger transaction the direct MCU→BQ path must satisfy its own 20 mV raw low margin at the selected open-drain drive and actual sink current. Review A-side 7.5–12.5 kΩ pulls against the DRV recommendation. |
| FUSB status and the selector are ordinary CMOS outputs | OUT1/2 are open-drain in GPIO operation; I²C-mode limits cannot be silently substituted. At the actual fanout use leakage against the weakest receiver plus Q807. SW400 is ~0.909 VDD high at nominal values with pulls disabled. Its contact evidence is at substantially higher current. | Keep the pull-up leakage requirement and switch low-current qualification. A reset 100 kΩ pull-down permits a different default-low proof than a generic MCU VOL bound. |
| A negative matrix worksheet number proves missed keystrokes | Five and four populated row loads use different current bounds. The vendor VF point at 1 mA and 25 °C does not guarantee cold forward drop at the actual current. | The DC limit and scan-settle proof remain open. All 67 nets and their normal reverse-stress screens were confirmed. |
| Zero supervisor threshold headroom can be rounded up | U901 RESET VOL(max) and U900 EN VIL(max) both reach 0.40 V under their valid supply/load conditions. | The raw forced-off margin is zero, before shared return drop. Establish a positive bound or change the inhibit circuit. |

Reviewers independently examined the component statements in [passives](passives.md), [logic levels](logic.md), [semiconductors](semiconductors.md) and the [supply-pin ledger](supply-pins.csv). Claims above are calculations, document comparisons or netlist observations. A missing guarantee has not been promoted to a physical failure.

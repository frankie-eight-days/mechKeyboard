# Rev F logic-level audit

This audit covers static levels, loading, pull networks, startup and split-power conditions on the keyboard’s eleven schematic sheets. The associated pin-level ledger has **317 case rows** and accounts for every one of **270 connected XML signal pins** declared as an input, output, bidirectional pin or open collector on U, Q, LED or J200 parts. It includes all 67 switch/diode paths and all 66 pixel-to-pixel hops. Grounds, supplies, unused pads and anonymous passives are traced to the companion pin and supply records. Additional rows identify relevant analog nodes, passive ESD interfaces and external breakout/probe endpoints. [Pin-level logic ledger](logic-levels.csv) · [KiCad schematic](../../electronics/keyboard-schematic.pdf)

The baseline is the live KiCad 10.0.8 XML export with root and controller Rev F, SHA-256 `baa9af9948d38688b21471ea33878fb2ecfbe241a403e98ff258ce29caa41a5e`. A scripted check traced the actual switch/diode segments, pixel chain and same-net edges, and checked uniqueness of state/pin combinations. Analog functions and known NC mode straps remain visible in the ledger. A receptacle or header pad appearing as a driver or receiver represents an external device through that physical terminal. The Adafruit panel pin numbers come from its Rev B assembly schematic.

## Interpreting the margins

The high margin is (V_{OH,min}-V_{IH,min}). The low margin is (V_{IL,max}-V_{OL,max}). Blank cells identify unknown bounds or a signal with no binary threshold. The `basis`, `condition_and_load` and `closure` columns identify the calculation and its scope. An engineering comparison may have a negative signed margin without proving a particular board fails. A zero datasheet margin leaves no allowance for threshold variation beyond the specific guarantee, trace loss, local ground offset or noise.

The working 3V3 points of 3.2505 V and 3.3495 V apply the regulator’s quoted ±1.5% at its test point. They do not establish the actual ripple, voltage, thermal or startup envelope at each pin. Nordic same-rail margins correlate the source and receiver at the low point, so (V_{IH}=2.27535) V and (V_{IL}=0.97515) V. The generic standard-drive comparison is (V_{OH}=2.8505) V minimum and (V_{OL}=0.40) V maximum at the 0.5 mA test load. Nordic separately states at least 1 mA standard-drive current at a 0.4 V pad drop. Neither entry is a general large-load or fast-transient bound. Keep temperature, current and voltage conditions associated with the relevant device rows. The loose standard-GPIO output-low table is a different case from the hardware default. Each enable is hard pulled to GND through 100 kΩ and its receiver has a low-region smart pulldown. At nRF reset, GPIO direction is input, the input buffer is disconnected and the pull is disabled. There is no external positive driver in those states. Ignoring the receiver’s helpful pulldown, the required combined upward leakage bounds with 101 kΩ maximum are **3.465 µA** for UI_EN and **2.970 µA** for HAPTIC_EN, before any local ground offset or noise. TI’s input leakage tables describe selected high/bias conditions, and the Nordic GPIO static table omits a numeric pad leakage limit. The direction of the topology supports a default low. It does not provide a numerical all-corners positive leakage bound, including when the inputs or rails power up asymmetrically. Driving low or releasing an unpulled Hi-Z output can be qualified separately. As a screening option, 10 kΩ would multiply the leakage allowance by ten and consume under 0.34 mA at the 3V3 high scenario when asserted. ## Calculation notes and boundary cases

### Matrix and physical controls

At the selected 3.2505 V rail, a row crossing 2.27535 V with the minimum 11 kΩ pull draws 0.207 mA. Five signal loads require 1.034 mA at the threshold, and four require 0.827 mA. C11/C12 each have four keys and are constrained to standard drive under 10 kHz. The lower-current unknown is diode Vf across temperature. (2.8505-2.27535=0.57515) V remains for the diode, switch, traces and ground offset. A 47 kΩ external row pull would reduce the five-row threshold load to 0.242 mA. It would also increase settling time and sensitivity to reverse leakage from other diodes and require translating the physical values to firmware. With the current five row pulls, BLE key-wake held keys can consume several tenths of a milliampere over multiple asserted rows. The firmware should handle an already held switch before re-entering idle. [JSCJ 1N4148W data sheet](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/a039f08bad6041678a289b80dd5d701f.pdf?productCode=C2099) · [Nordic GPIO](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/gpio.html) · [BMD-340 pin notes](https://content.u-blox.com/sites/default/files/BMD-340_DataSheet_UBX-19033353.pdf)

For MODE_SEL high, the 99 kΩ minimum pulldown through R400 and 10.1 kΩ maximum high-side R401 give 2.9496 V before contact/leakage losses. Enabling an internal nRF 11–16 kΩ pulldown makes high approximately 0.50–0.58 VDD, below the input guarantee. Open contact defaults low through R400. Test both stable mechanical positions, the transition and a failed contact without assuming the archival generic switch document proves actual make/break action or useful dry-circuit contact resistance. RV1000 is separate. Its ratiometric wiper/RC and nRF SAADC acquisition belong to the analog path. [Shou Han MSK12C02 electrical and mechanical specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/5162155576bfd231c35aa9a893d25c8c.pdf?productCode=C431540) · [Nordic SAADC](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/saadc.html)

### CC, USB data and USB monitor

FUSB303B Tables 1 and 8 encode sink mode status using open-drain release/pull-low. The output-specific 0.8/0.2×VDD push-pull entries belong to PORT/DEBUG and ADDR/ORIENT, and the SDA/OUT1 and SCL/OUT2 voltage/current section is explicitly I²C mode. The two 150 kΩ pulls need an aggregate release bound including the FUSB output, nRF input, HC input, Q807 gate leakage on OUT1 and PCB leakage. At the 3V3 low scenario and 151.5 kΩ maximum pull, the nRF high allows 6.44 µA before offsets, the adopted HC comparison allows about 4.88 µA using separate high/low rail extrema, and the Q807 2.5 V resistance-screen target allows about 4.95 µA. Capture actual high and low under both orientations, all four response codes, startup, detach and downgrade. A FET’s minimum threshold cannot establish the small permitted OFF drain current. [onsemi FUSB303B Tables 1 and 5–9](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/fusb303b-d.pdf) · [AO3400A static characteristics](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3400A.pdf)

Nexperia 74HC123 and 74HC238 give voltage tests at 2.0, 4.5 and 6.0 V, with light-load VOH/VOL rows at ±20 µA. A screen at the actual 3V3 rail uses 0.75×VDD high and 0.25×VDD low and conditionally uses VCC−0.1/0.1 V at the sources. It is a comparison, not a tabulated 3.3 V guarantee. HCT uses different inputs and a different recommended supply. Its thresholds do not apply. Some outputs have >20 µA load even in steady state and considerably more while charging the tier timing capacitors. The tier RC steady gate screen requires at least 2.755 V from each HC238 output before input current and gate return shifts. A cold or transitional driver output needs independent proof. At 25 °C, a resistor-only RC with ~9.1 kΩ Thevenin and 100 nF, 2.85 V final gate, reaches 2.5 V in about 1.9 ms. Actual operation can start earlier at lower VGS for a low-current branch. When falling, the fast path includes a silicon forward drop and neither its reached current-limiter resistance nor its release time follows from nominal RC alone. [Nexperia 74HC123 §§8–11](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT123.pdf) · [Nexperia 74HC238 §§8–10](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT238.pdf)

MAX809STRG holds LOGIC_READY low across its specified threshold and timeout; the table tests outputs at (V_{TH}pm0.2) V. Even the 2.85 V specified low threshold for −40…85 °C is at FUSB’s recommended operating minimum. Brownout propagation delay is typical at its test. Rail startup/brownout can transiently enter combinations where no active FUSB status output guarantee applies. HC timer 1 reset is tied high. Nexperia allows a power-up pulse, and the design relies on actual D-line behavior for active/quiet cutoff and a second independent configured SOF pulse. Verify both timer widths, legitimate and unintended retriggers, POR and slow ramps in the same trace as R800/MAIN_ON and the current-tier gates. [onsemi MAX809STRG electrical characteristics](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/max809s-d.pdf) · [Nexperia 74HC123 §11.2](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT123.pdf)

USB full-speed has 2.8–3.6 V driven high, ≤0.3 V driven low, 2.0/0.8 V single-ended receiver thresholds and 0.2 V differential sensitivity in its common-mode window. U803 is a separate single-ended receiver, tied through R807/R817 1 kΩ taps. A valid driven 3.6 V can exceed the HC recommended VIN upper bound of its own VCC even when nominally powered. If the logic rail has collapsed, driven D+ or D− can source milliamps through the tap and input clamp. The 1 kΩ tap need not fail its absolute clamp-current limit to alter rail retention. The direct pair also omits the BMD reference’s 27 Ω positions. Establish line routability/series tuning and scope actual USBMON thresholds and EOP operation over the power trajectories. U200:4 VBUS-good rising threshold can be as high as 4.48 V. USB_VBUS at the BMD PHY is separate and demands ≥4.35 V before operation. ### Charger and haptic I²C

At the selected resistor corners, R100/R101 4.7 kΩ in parallel with the TCA9416’s 7.5–12.5 kΩ internal B-side pull is 2.87–3.44 kΩ. A low at 0.40 V draws about 1.03 mA on the B bus with V3V3=3.3495 V and minimum pull resistance. Another 0.19 mA is possible from the 1.8 V A pull through the pass path. Available current and VOL depend on pin drive selection, sink location, translator accelerator state, test voltage and the DC drop through the translator. With haptics disabled, the board pull alone still draws about 0.63 mA at 0.4 V before leakage. A deliberate nRF open-drain high-drive configuration for relevant pins is possible, but still requires proof against BQ’s narrow VIL. With DRV asserting SDA at its permitted 0.40 V, BQ accepts only 20 mV more at its receiver before ground/noise allowance. A simple (20	ext{ mV}/1.03	ext{ mA}) target is roughly 19.5 Ω. A representative 25 Ω adds 26 mV. The vendor’s 25 Ω guarantee is at a different input bias (0.2 V) and specified supply pairs. The MCU receives the ACK on a normal haptic transaction and has substantially more static headroom. BQ is not addressed in that transaction. Its loaded threshold still matters for shared-bus compatibility and faults, particularly what the charger sees as START/STOP or bus stuck low. A haptic ACK projected 6 mV above the BQ limit is neither a failed MCU ACK nor a demonstrated fault on a non-addressed BQ. Internal TCA RTA/FTA thresholds control edge accelerators. They are distinct from digital node acceptance levels. [TI TCA9416 §§6.5,7.3,9.2](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9416.pdf) · [TI BQ25622 §§7.5,8.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf) · [TI DRV2605L §§6.3,6.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv2605l.pdf)

The haptic translator is isolated under stated both-powered OE-low and zero-rail tests. Those tests do not bound crossings through a partially collapsed rail. R1101/C1108 nominal 10 ms allows OE to linger on loss of the 1.8 V supply. Check rising and falling TCA UVLO, DRV EN and DRV VDD, of interest during SYS collapse with 3V3 alive under USB retention. Use the applicable Ioff row at exact tested voltage and report A/B-port conditions rather than a blanket isolation guarantee. BQ25622 CHG_INT_N and charger data/clock remain pulled up while the core can be alive without the charger powered. BQ supports a powered interface reference range, and its absolute 6 V digital limit is not an Ioff guarantee. Include absent-pack and main-switch-off tests with monitored current and bus line state. [TI TCA9416 §6.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9416.pdf) · [TI BQ25622 §§7.5,8.3.9,8.5.1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

### RGB data and safety gate

The first-pixel link passes through U300 and R301 33 Ω. R302 100 kΩ biases inactive DIN. A 50 µA nominal DC R302 load drops 1.65 mV across R301. The receiver criterion at 5.0 V/25 °C is 3.25 V high and 1.50 V low. TI guarantees power-off Ioff for the translator at the stated port conditions, and the 100 kΩ pull then contributes at most about 0.202 V from that leakage alone, before DIN or PCB leakage. During LED rail ramp neither the valid-power thresholds nor the zero-rail Ioff condition alone proves absence of pulses or injected current. Verify MOSI stays low until the rail and translator settle. Across the chain, source output high and output low are unspecified by the exact 12 mA device sheet at all local power distributions of interest. Repeat the bound at the first, most ground-shifted and final ends. The 25 ns nominal T0H timing margin after SPI encoding is a separate issue to verify at DIN. [TI SN74LVC1T45 §§5.3,5.5,7.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1t45.pdf) · [OPSCO B/2 §§9–10](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/0907a34a701b4aef568078003f6e9eae.pdf?productCode=C5149201)

The supervisor’s 0.4 V maximum low is at 1 mA and its valid supply conditions. R904 contributes about 0.30 mA when U100 requests high and U901 asserts low. This fits the clamp test current, but TPS61252’s maximum acceptable low is itself 0.40 V. At still-lower SYS, the supervisor's power-on-reset low entry has different load and slew conditions. RGB_PG is a gated pull-up via 100 kΩ R906; with 3V3 active, gate enabled and PG released, the screen is about 2.525 V before an nRF input leakage allowance or local ground/noise. An actual released-high aggregate sink much greater than about 2.8 µA could fail the receiver. Internally pulled GPIO modes are inappropriate. Boost shutdown releases the open-drain PG output. R906 pulls it toward the gated input state rather than directly up to a continuously powered rail. [TI TPS3808 §§6.5–6.6](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf) · [TI TPS61252 §§6.5,8.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61252.pdf)

### Charger interlock and OLED

At VBUS_LIMITED 3.9 V the fitted R604/R605 divider has a minimum unloaded CE of 1.9305 V. At the active CE threshold 0.78 V, about 23 µA downward current is permitted at resistor corners before leakage/noise. At the 0.40 V required CE low with 5.5 V applied, the Q600/Q601 stack must sink about 47.6 µA from the network plus leakage/bias. Equivalent series resistance at about 48.6 µA must be ≤8.24 kΩ before further margin. There is no specified JSCJ ON resistance at actual Q601 gate voltage or guaranteed OFF drain leakage at any nonzero residual VGS. Compare the dual TLV7042’s 0.35 V maximum low at 5 V/3 mA against its real REGN and R610 100 kΩ; that test is not a cold ramp or temperature-window qualification. Constrain charge software to enable only after safe readback. [TI TLV7042 dual §5.9](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv7042.pdf) · [JSCJ 2N7002 electrical table](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/a141b8bd86b14475955ac8c4d3eea0a8.pdf?productCode=C8545) · [TI BQ25622 §7.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25622.pdf)

The Adafruit 4440 Rev B circuit shows a 10 kΩ upstream pull on each module input, 10 kΩ on each panel side, and a dual BSS138 level-shifting circuit. R1002/R1003 create approximately 5 kΩ on the connector side while the cable and panel are attached. A textbook external standard-mode 1 µs limit does not close the OLED internal data path. The SSD1306 I²C table stipulates ≤300 ns rise and fall at the panel under its stated 25 °C test. A 10 kΩ local pull alone allows approximately 35.4 pF for that edge, with further effects from FET coupling. The SSD1306 output-low table gives 0.1VDD at 100 µA. When the panel acknowledges, the nominal static pull-up network can source roughly 0.85–0.90 mA, even before cable and level-shift characterization. That is well outside the tabulated output-load point. Verify actual ACK voltage across the cable, timing and panel voltage against the acquired module. U1000 power switches the entire display; the motherboard contains no bus isolation. Open-drain release with MCU pulls off is part of boot, sleep, reconnect and service sequencing. ## Gate disposition

The pin-level coverage passes. The logic support for USB source tiers, PMIC/haptic ACK, the RGB undervoltage clamp, the selected matrix drive and the OLED interface remains open at the conditions described above. The quantified comparisons guide schematic decisions. Layout-dependent traces, return offsets, RF effects, USB eye and D-line monitor timing, resistor/capacitor parasitics, explicit programming probe choice and firmware implementation require their own verification. In particular, a passing ERC or a positive margin at nominal supply does not close arbitrary power-domain transitions or establish fault coverage.

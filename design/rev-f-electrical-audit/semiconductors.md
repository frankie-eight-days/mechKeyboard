# Rev F semiconductor junction and interface audit

This desk audit uses the frozen Rev F KiCad XML with SHA-256 `baa9af9948d38688b21471ea33878fb2ecfbe241a403e98ff258ce29caa41a5e`, the assembly references and the exact-part documents in [datasheets/index.csv](../../datasheets/index.csv). The [CSV ledger](semiconductors.csv) covers **153 fitted references and 157 discrete/device rows**, plus **17 selected IC protection or intrinsic paths**, for **174 rows total**. It enumerates 67 matrix diodes, D802/D803, both junctions in each BAS40-05, 13 MOSFETs with their body-diode paths, four TVS channels and 67 addressable LED devices with every digital predecessor and successor. The intrinsic rows address documented cross-domain and fault paths. Vendor proprietary on-chip junctions remain outside the available component evidence. No hardware measurements were available.

Positive margin means room remaining against the cited limit. Negative margin identifies the cited screen and condition. An assumed stimulus, published typical point, graph, resistance bound or test-condition extrapolation stays conditional in the ledger. This is the placement audit for Rev F. Production acceptance requires the physical checks identified below.

## Grouping and audit checks

- Every matrix diode has its unique switch, row, column and actual U100 pin path in the CSV. The five row populations are 15, 15, 14, 14 and 9. Column loads are five for C0–C8, four for C9–C13 and two for C14. A low row is distinct from a high row whose unselected diodes sink reverse leakage. Standard and high-drive arguments are separate and respect the restricted BMD pads.
- Q600 and Q601 are individually mapped. At 57 µA an effective series path ≤7.02 kΩ satisfies 0.4 V CE low. JSCJ guarantees `RDS(on)` at higher drive, so actual low-gate and hot/cold conditions remain open. The MOS body paths face away from sinking the positive CE pullup. The nine AO3400A applications have individual pin maps, branch current cases, off-state paths and conditional SOA. Their DC channel load ranges from about 0.056 mA on the ON branch to a conservative 8.675 mA upper screen for Q800’s ILIM branch using 5.5 V at the resistor. The normal absolute stress screen leaves +24.5 V VDS and ≥+6.5 V for both VGS polarities. Tier switching, gate leakage, partial-power pulls and body conduction still require functional tests.
- The LED order was traced by DIN/DOUT endpoints from U300 through R301, follows five actual reversible row runs, and terminates at TP300. No generic LED VF or standalone diode PIV/SOA has been assumed for any smart pixel. All circuit references were checked for exact equality against the XML, including the separate D800/D801 anodes and the U201/U202 pad 3 and pad 5 channels. Physical polarity, reverse-mount paste land verification and pulse/compliance testing remain layout or bench checks.

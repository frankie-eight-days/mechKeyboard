# Volume and display, revision E

A 10 kΩ linear potentiometer on the top right controls the selected host’s volume. A removable 128 × 32 white OLED in the middle of the rear shelf shows two lines of information. The display and knob have separate power controls so the volume still works with the screen dark. Firmware behavior below is the interface design for the firmware stage. [Schematic, sheet 10](../electronics/keyboard-schematic.pdf) · [Enclosure and placement](fit.md)

## Turning the dial

RV1000 is the vertical, single-gang Alps Alpine RK09K1130A6S. It has a flatted shaft, 280° ±5° mechanical travel, no center click and 1–8 mN·m running torque. The 22 mm direct-drive dial gives a tangential force of approximately 0.09–0.73 N at its rim. An index mark makes position legible. The printable knob has a hidden groove for an optional 11 mm ID × 1.5 mm silicone O-ring. Light radial compression against the lid adds drag. The additional torque depends on material and print tolerance. Human feel and print-to-shaft fit remain unmeasured. Both mechanical tabs should be soldered. [Alps dimensional drawing, operating torque and soldering instructions](https://tech.alpsalpine.com/e/products/detail/RK09K1130A6S/) · [Main-board potentiometer](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C209770.html)

POT_EXC drives the clockwise end of the track. The wiper feeds AIN4 through 1 kΩ and 10 nF. The maximum nominal source resistance is 3.5 kΩ at mid-travel, or 4 kΩ allowing +20% track resistance. That gives a time constant of 40 µs before capacitor tolerance. A 2 ms excitation pulse leaves ample RC settling time. Select the SAADC’s VDD/4 reference and 1/4 gain for a nominal VDD full scale, take settled samples at 20 Hz, filter chatter and use endpoint calibration. Return POT_EXC low after sampling. Suppress events on boot, reconnection and transport changes until the user physically turns the dial. [Nordic SAADC](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf52840/page/saadc.html) · [Power budget](power-budget.md)

The firmware target is standard HID Consumer Volume Increment and Decrement on the active USB or BLE host. Accumulate knob motion across hysteresis bands, send a press and release per step, and clear pending steps on a host change. Operating systems choose the step size and can change volume independently. This mode shows direction or knob position on the OLED. An optional companion can instead map absolute knob position to 0–100% system volume and return the actual host level. Enable only one volume-control mode at a time. [USB-IF HID Usage Tables, Consumer page](https://www.usb.org/hid)

## Display and host data

Adafruit 4440 uses an SSD1306 at fixed 7-bit address 0x3C. A straight-through four-position JST-SH cable carries GND, switched 3.3 V, SDA and SCL on J1000 pins 1 through 4. The main PCB uses the XYECONN XY-SM04B-SRSS-TB side-entry header, with its own land pattern and 3D model. Its pitch, pin order and mechanical envelope were compared to the SH interface. Connector-to-cable retention remains unmeasured. Supply comes from U1000. Run the display on TWIM1 at 100 kHz and reserve TWIM0 for the charger. The OLED and main-board pull-ups both reference switched power. Disable internal pulls, release SDA/SCL and stop TWIM1 before removing display power. On a wake, enable the rail, wait for the module's power-on reset, then initialize the SSD1306 again. A stuck-low or unplugged display should time out without blocking scanning, HID or charging. [Display and mating connection](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4440) · [TI load switch](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22919.pdf)

### Voltage at the connector and glass

U700 generates the keyboard’s 3.3 V logic supply. U1000 (TPS22919) is a series load switch. With UI_EN asserted and the rail settled, its output follows its input less I × RON. The switched net is labeled **OLED_VIN_SW**. TI gives 90 mΩ typical at 3.6 V and 25 °C. At the 35 mA display allowance this corresponds to **3.15 mV**. A conservative screening resistance of 0.40 Ω gives **14 mV**. This borrows the published 1.8 V, −40 to +85 °C limit because TI does not tabulate a 3.3 V limit. The charge-up ramp is a separate transient. [TPS22919 §6.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22919.pdf)

For a steady-state example, applying the TPS63900’s ±1.5% DC accuracy at its specified 1 mA test point gives 3.2505 V on the low side. Allowing 0.40 Ω for U1000 and a provisional 0.15 Ω total for traces, both cable conductors and contacts gives **3.231 V at the breakout** at 35 mA. This estimate excludes load regulation away from the test point, converter ripple and wiring variation. [TPS63900 electrical characteristics](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps63900.pdf)

On Adafruit’s I²C rev B schematic, J1000 pin 2 ultimately supplies an AP2112K-3.3 LDO on the breakout. Its output supplies **both** LCD1 pin 8 (logic VDD) and pin 5 (charge-pump VBAT). The LDO cannot maintain 3.300 V from a 3.231 V input. Diodes specifies typical/maximum dropout of 5/8 mV at 10 mA and 125/200 mV at 300 mA under its table conditions. It gives no maximum at our exact 35 mA load. Adafruit describes running the assembled module from nominal 3.3 V. The UG-2832HSWEG02 panel identified in its CAD specifies **VBAT ≥3.3 V** with the internal pump enabled, and **VDD ≤3.3 V** for logic operation. Accordingly, full-range operation to the bare-panel limits is **unverified**. A higher breakout input would give the LDO headroom but its output tolerance still straddles 3.3 V. Closing this item requires a specified operating envelope for the actual assembled display covering our minimum connector voltage, or a display supply/interface whose guaranteed limits match the fitted panel. ### Frame updates

One monochrome framebuffer occupies 512 bytes. At 100 kHz a full frame takes at least 46 ms including I²C ACK bits, before commands and transaction overhead. Updating changed rows at up to 5 Hz leaves time for other firmware work. Both boards have 10 kΩ pull-ups in parallel, giving roughly 5 kΩ per line and a 30–70% rise time of about 0.42 µs at 100 pF. Keep the short internal cable and both boards within the standard-mode 1 µs limit, corresponding to about 236 pF for this resistance. Blank the display during unqualified USB startup and suspend. The OLED brightness/content budget and switched-rail turn-on current are in the power record. [I²C bus specification](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/user-guide/UM10204.pdf) · [Board power budget](power-budget.md)

Locally available status includes the active transport, BLE connection state, battery voltage and charger status. For a game dashboard, the selected host supplies named metrics, for example kills and assists from a game integration or frame rate from a performance overlay. A game-specific reader and USB/BLE companion belong to the firmware/software stage. A compact first protocol can carry two fields per update using a 20-byte payload (version, sequence, and two nine-byte printable-ASCII fields) on vendor HID over USB or a writable GATT characteristic on the connected, bonded BLE host. Limit to five updates per second, clear the last host’s fields on transport change, and fall back to local status after five seconds without an update. This keeps the keyboard usable with no companion and avoids showing stale stats as live.
